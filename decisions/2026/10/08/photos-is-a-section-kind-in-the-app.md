# Photos is a Section kind in the app

Photos is a fifth Section kind, guessed for a ticked folder that is mostly dated pictures, or chosen
by the person, so any folder on any drive can be one, not only the one folder `decisions/2026/10/04/an-event-is-the-work-in-photos.md` was written
for. Its Works are Events (`decisions/2026/10/04/an-event-is-the-work-in-photos.md`, with `decisions/2026/10/04/photos-take-no-marks-review-is-the-progress.md` for marks): a Photo's date comes from its file
name, then EXIF, never the file's modified time, and an Event's id is the Section id, `#` and its
first Photo's path inside the Section without the extension, so the same name in two subfolders
stays two ids. Folders inside a Photos Section do not show: years and months take the place of the
folder columns, and Events gather Photos from every subfolder. Review choices are kept per Photo in
StoredData and go into the marks backup. Photos comes in two steps: Browse (Events, the calendar,
cards, the Event dialog, the viewer, On this day), then Sort (Copies, Similar shots, the Best one,
Junk, Review and the move scripts of `decisions/2026/10/04/a-move-script-for-each-system.md`).

## Considered Options

- Photos only for one named folder, as the Hub planned: no guess to get wrong, but a person with a
  second photo folder, or a friend with their own, could not use it.
- Folders inside a Photos Section as columns above the years: keeps a hand-made order, but a phone
  dump has none worth keeping, and two levels of navigation for one kind of content is one too many.
